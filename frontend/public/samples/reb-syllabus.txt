S2 Mathematics syllabus (sample)
Subject: Mathematics
Class: Senior 2

Unit 1: Numbers and operations
Lessons: integers, fractions, percentages.
Learning outcomes:
- Compare integers and place them on a number line.
- Add and subtract fractions with the same denominator.
- Calculate a percentage of a quantity.

Unit 2: Algebra
Lessons: algebraic expressions, simple equations.
Learning outcomes:
- Simplify an expression by collecting like terms.
- Solve a linear equation with one unknown.
- Check a solution by substitution.

Unit 3: Geometry
Lessons: angles, triangles, perimeter.
Learning outcomes:
- Name and measure acute, right, and obtuse angles.
- Find a missing angle in a triangle.
- Calculate the perimeter of a rectangle and a triangle.

Assessment: short exercise at the end of each lesson, and a weekly quiz.
Resources: textbook, ruler, protractor, chalkboard.
